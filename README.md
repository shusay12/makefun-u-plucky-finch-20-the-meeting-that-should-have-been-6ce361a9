# The Meeting That Should Have Been an Email

One original one-liner (113 characters) about a meeting that should have been an email:

> Somewhere, an unsent email is sitting in on this meeting and thinking: 'I could have done this in two sentences.'

The joke personifies the unsent email as a fellow attendee — it sat through the same meeting and knows it only needed two sentences. It lands on its own, with the twist arriving at the last word, and names something everyone who has sat in that meeting recognises.
